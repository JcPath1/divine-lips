PUT YOUR VIDEO HERE!
====================

When you and Dad finish filming your lip gloss video:

1. Save the video file in THIS folder (mockup-site/videos/)
2. Name it exactly:  glimmerbliss-ad.mp4
   (all lowercase, with the dashes, ending in .mp4)
3. Open the website (index.html) and press Ctrl + Shift + R

Your video will play automatically on the welcome screen!

Tip: Most phones save videos as .mp4 or .mov.
If yours is a .mov file, ask Dad to rename it to .mp4
(or tell Claude and it can help convert it).
