Meet the Founders

Divine Lips was born from a friendship and a shared love of all things beauty. We're Charlie and Martina, two best friends who love doing makeup, hair, and skincare.

We started making lip glosses as a fun project, and everyone kept asking where they could buy them. So we decided to share our creations with the world!

Every gloss is handmade by us with love and care. We pick the flavors, mix the colors, and pack each order ourselves. When you buy from Divine Lips, you're supporting two young entrepreneurs!
