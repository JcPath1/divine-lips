Returns & Refunds

Last updated: add the date you publish this

The short version

We want you to love your gloss. If something is wrong with your order, tell us and we'll make it right. But because lip gloss touches your lips, we can't take back a gloss that has been opened or used - that's a health and safety rule, not us being mean.

What we CAN take back

  • Unopened glosses, still sealed, within 14 days of you receiving them.
  • Damaged or broken items - if it arrives leaking, cracked or squashed, we'll replace it or refund you.
  • Wrong item - if we sent the wrong color or flavor, that's on us. We'll send the right one, free.
  • Never arrived - see our Shipping Policy.

What we CAN'T take back

  • Any gloss that has been opened or used, for hygiene reasons.
  • Limited Edition items, unless they arrive damaged or wrong.
  • Anything sent back after 14 days.
  • Orders where you simply changed your mind after opening it.

A quick note on Glimmer Kiss: it turns a different pink on everybody, because it reacts to your own natural pH. Getting a different shade than your friend is the gloss working correctly, not a fault - so it isn't a reason for a refund.

How to start a return

  1. Email hello@glimmerbliss.com within 14 days of getting your order.
  2. Tell us your order number and what went wrong. Photos help a lot, especially for damage.
  3. We'll reply within 2 business days and tell you what to do next.

Please don't post anything back before you hear from us - we may not need it returned at all.

Who pays the postage?

If the mistake or damage was ours, we pay. If you're returning an unopened gloss because you changed your mind, the return postage is yours to cover.

When do I get my money back?

Once we've received the return or seen your photos, refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

Local pickup orders

Please check your glosses when you collect them. It's much easier to swap something out on the spot than by post.

Still stuck?

Email hello@glimmerbliss.com. We're a very small shop run by a kid and her dad, and we'd much rather fix a problem than have an unhappy customer.
