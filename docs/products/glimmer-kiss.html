How does it work?

Everyone's lips are a little different. They have their own natural moisture and chemistry, called pH. The color-changing ingredient in Glimmer Kiss reacts with your lips, so it develops into a pink that's all yours. Some people get a soft, barely-there pink, and others get a brighter pink. Put it on next to a friend and you'll probably end up with two different pinks.
