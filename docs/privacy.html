Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Glimmer Bliss is a small handmade lip gloss shop run by Charlie Mae. Her mom and dad help with the grown-up parts. This page says what we keep about you, why, and what you can ask us to do.

What we ask for, and why

When you order: your name, email, and phone number (the phone is optional). If we are shipping, we also need your street address, city, state and ZIP. You can add an optional note. We use this to make your order, send you receipts, and get it to you or arrange pickup. We also keep what you ordered, what you paid, how you chose to pay, and your order code.

When you write a review: your first name, your review, and an optional email that is never shown. We read every review first. If we post it, it shows your first name and sometimes one letter (like "Emma S."), the gloss, and the stars. Your email is never shown.

When you use My Orders: there is no password. Type your email and we send a one-time login link. We keep that link for 20 minutes.

Cookies and your browser

Your cart is saved inside your own browser, on your own device. We do not see it until you place an order.

One cookie is set if you log in to My Orders, so the site remembers you for up to 30 days. We have no advertising cookies and no analytics tools on our site.

To slow down spam and guessing, we note your device's internet address (IP) when someone tries to log in, send a review, or order. We keep those notes only to count recent tries, and we delete the old ones. Like any website, our hosting company may keep its own standard technical logs of visits.

Who else sees it

Our web host (Spaceship) stores the shop's database and sends our emails from orders@glimmerbliss.com.

Venmo. If you pay with Venmo, you pay in the Venmo app and Venmo handles it. Our website never sees or keeps your card number or Venmo login. If you pay by card at our booth, Venmo processes it. We see the amount and the card type and last four digits on Venmo's receipt, never the full number.

Delivery. We use your name and address to ship with USPS.

One outside file. The page that shows our payment QR code loads a small free QR tool from Cloudflare's cdnjs, so that service can see your device's internet address. Everything else on our site (pictures, videos, fonts) comes from our own server.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep order information for as long as we need it to run the shop, help if something goes wrong, and keep our records (for example for taxes). We have not set a fixed time yet.

Your choices

You can ask us what we have about you, ask us to fix it, or ask us to delete it. Email orders@glimmerbliss.com from the email address you used and we'll get back to you within 2 business days. We may need to keep some order records, for example for taxes.

Kids

The shop is made for grown-ups to order from. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before you order or write a review. We do not ask for anyone's age. If a child under 13 sent us information without a parent's OK, a parent can email us and we will delete it.

Keeping it safe

The whole site uses HTTPS (the padlock). Order details sit in a private database, and only the shop's owners can open them, with a login that locks after too many wrong tries. No website can promise perfect security, but we take care of what you trust us with.

Changes

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and the date at the top. Questions? See our FAQ or email orders@glimmerbliss.com.
