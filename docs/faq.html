About the glosses

What flavor are your glosses?

Every single batch is made with vanilla flavoring. There's no unflavored option - they're all vanilla.

Are they safe for my lips?

Yes. Every gloss is made with lip-safe, cosmetic-grade ingredients, including cosmetic glitter - never craft glitter, which should never go near your mouth. If you have allergies, email us at orders@glimmerbliss.com for the full ingredient list before you order.

How big is a bottle?

Each bottle holds 5ml. It stands 3.31 inches tall and is 0.87 inches wide - about the size of your thumb. It has a white square cap and a wide, soft wand. There's a photo showing the exact size on every gloss page.

How does Glimmer Kiss change color?

It goes on completely clear, then turns a soft pink on your lips. The exact shade comes from your own natural pH, which is slightly different for everyone - so your pink is genuinely yours. Two friends wearing it side by side will end up with two different pinks.

Why is Glimmer Kiss more expensive?

It's $15 instead of $12 because it's Limited Edition and uses a special color-changing base plus cosmetic glitter. Only a few are made, and when they're gone, they're gone.

Will the color look the same on me?

Close, but lip glosses always look a little different on different people. Glimmer Kiss is designed to be different on purpose. Photos on a screen can also shift color slightly.

Is it sticky?

No - the finish is glossy and lightweight, meant to feel smooth rather than tacky.

How long does a gloss last before it goes off?

Use it within 12 months of opening. Keep it out of hot cars and direct sunlight, and put the cap back on properly.

Do you test on animals?

Never. Every gloss is mixed by hand by Charlie Mae in small batches.

Shipping & pickup

How much is shipping?

A flat $7.00 - and it's free when you spend $35 or more, which is three glosses. Full details on the Shipping page.

Can I pick up instead of paying for shipping?

Yes! Local pickup is free. Choose it at checkout and we'll message you to arrange a safe public meet-up spot, or pickup at our booth.

How long will my order take?

Glosses are made to order, so allow 3 to 5 business days to make and pack, then 2 to 5 business days in the post.

Do you ship outside the US?

Not at the moment - we ship within the US only. If you're further away, message us and we'll see what we can do.

Can I track my order?

Yes - you'll get a tracking number by email as soon as the label is printed.

Do you charge sales tax?

Yes, for Texas orders. Local pickup and orders shipped to a Texas address pay 8.25% Texas sales tax. Orders shipped to other states don't pay Texas tax. You'll see the exact amount at checkout before you order.

Returns

Can I return a gloss or get a refund?

No - all sales are final. Lip gloss goes on your lips, so for everyone's safety we don't take returns, give refunds, or do exchanges. See the All Sales Final page.

My gloss arrived broken. What do I do?

Email orders@glimmerbliss.com with your order code and a photo within 7 days and we'll send you a replacement. No need to mail it back.

I got the wrong color. Help!

Sorry! Email us your order code and a photo within 7 days and we'll send the right one free.
