Meet Charlie Mae: Founder, Gloss Maker, Age 11

Glimmer Bliss is Charlie Mae's business. It was her idea, the recipes are hers, and she has her hands on every bottle.

She has always loved makeup and hair, and she's the kid who reads the back of every skincare label. So she decided to make her own lip gloss. Her first batches didn't go well: some were too sticky, and some were too runny. She kept testing and adjusting until she made one she loved. Then her friends wanted their own, and Glimmer Bliss was born.

Today Charlie runs the creative side of the business herself:

  • She chooses every color, name, and flavor
  • She measures and hand-mixes every batch
  • She packs every order that ships
  • She works her own booth at First Monday Trade Days in Canton, Texas, where she helps customers find their shade

Her mom and dad help with the grown-up parts. Everything else is Charlie.

When you buy a Glimmer Bliss gloss, it doesn't come from a factory. You're supporting a young entrepreneur learning to build a real business, and you get a gloss she made herself.

Thank you for supporting a small business with a big dream.
