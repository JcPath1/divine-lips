Meet the Founder

Hi! I'm Charlie Mae, and Glimmer Bliss is mine. I have always loved doing makeup and hair, and I'm the girl who reads the back of every skincare bottle to see what's really inside it.

One day I wondered if I could make a lip gloss myself instead of just buying one. My first tries were messy. Some were too sticky, some were too runny, and a few went straight in the bin. But I kept going until I had one I loved - and then my friends kept asking me to make them one too.

So I turned it into a real little business. I pick every color and flavor, mix each batch by hand, and pack every order myself. When you buy a Glimmer Bliss gloss, you're not buying it from a big factory - you're buying it from me. Thank you for believing in a kid with a big idea!

Charlie Mae
