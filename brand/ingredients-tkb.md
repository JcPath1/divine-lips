775969, 19 Oct 2026) and each product's own page on tkbtrading.com, read 12 Nov 2026.

**This is a list of supplies, not a recipe.** It does not say which supplies go into which gloss, or how
much. A product label needs the ingredients of each finished gloss in order from most to least, so the
recipes are still needed before any label or ingredient claim is written.

## Other facts on TKB's pages

- **Vanilla Flavoring Oil:** shelf life 6 to 9 months; recommended use 2 to 3% by weight; "This product
  is synthetic"; the carrier is soybean oil.
- **Versagel ME base:** shelf life stated as "2-3 years" in one place and "3 years" in another.
- **Color Changing Lip Base:** no shelf life stated; "manufactured in the USA"; can be used as is; TKB
  suggests up to 1 g of glitter per 10 g of gloss.
- **White Diamond Glitter:** cut smaller than 150 microns. The page shows nail use only and does not say
  lip safe. Its consumer warning reads: "While the FDA doesn't formally permit polyester glitter in
  cosmetics, the issue is under review and so they allow it." It also says "Product contains aluminum."
- **High Shine Top Coat:** TKB says "We do NOT recommend coloring this product with mica, pigments or
  glitter."
- **TKB order emails:** "Batch-specific COAs are not issued. Documentation is provided in standard form
  only."

## Open questions before any label or claim

1. **Recipes.** Which supplies, and how many grams or drops of each, go into each of the 5 glosses?
2. **Vitamin E.** The brand kit says vitamin E is "not used". The High Shine Top Coat and the Log Cabin
   and Yummy Pink Lip Liquids contain Tocopheryl Acetate (Vitamin E). Any gloss made with them has it
   and must list it.
3. **Glitter.** Is White Diamond Glitter the sparkle in Glimmer Kiss? If so, read TKB's warning above
   before listing it on TikTok Shop, which bars FDA-prohibited or restricted ingredients.
4. **Soy.** The vanilla oil is soybean oil based and every gloss is vanilla flavored.
5. **Other suppliers.** Only these two TKB orders were found in this Gmail account. Anything bought
   elsewhere is not on this list.
6. **Shelf life of the finished gloss.** Not established. The vanilla oil alone is 6 to 9 months.
7. **"May contain" colours.** TKB lists a pool of possible colours for Log Cabin and Yummy Pink. The
   SDS and COA linked on each TKB page were not read.
