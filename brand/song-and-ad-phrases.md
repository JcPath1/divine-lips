# Phrase bank for songs and short ads

Wording JC saved on 11 Nov 2026 (from two screenshots of the same list). These play on the words
**glimmer** and **bliss**. Check every line against `brand/brand-guidelines.md` section 4 before it goes
in a song or an ad. JC approves every post before it goes anywhere.

## OK to use as written

- "Built on bliss, shining with a glimmer."
- "Swipe on the glimmer, feel the sweet bliss, every single bottle made with magic like this."

## OK only for Glimmer Kiss, or as a play on the brand name

The brand kit says only Glimmer Kiss has sparkle, and "glimmer" must not describe another gloss. These
lines are fine in a Glimmer Kiss ad or in a brand song where "glimmer" and "bliss" clearly mean the name
Glimmer Bliss. Do not use them to describe any of the other four glosses.

- "One swipe of glimmer, a pocket full of bliss!"
- "Pure lip gloss bliss with a little bit of glimmer."
- "A little bit of glimmer, a whole lot of bliss, never found a lip gloss shining like this."
  (The second half compares us with other glosses. Keep it to songs, not ad claims.)
