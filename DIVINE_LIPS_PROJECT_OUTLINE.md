# Divine Lips - Project Outline

## The Story
Two best friends, Charlie and Martina, love doing hair and makeup at school. They're both into skincare and facial products, and decided to turn their passion into a real business. Divine Lips was born - handmade lip glosses with fun flavors and beautiful packaging.

## Fun Ideas to Explore

### 1. Mystery Gloss
- Customer doesn't know which flavor they'll get
- Lower price point, clears inventory, creates excitement
- "Trust us, you'll love it!"

### 2. Build Your Own Bundle
- Pick 3 glosses, get a discount
- Comes in special bundle box

### 3. Seasonal/Holiday Editions
- **Summer:** Tropical flavors, bright colors
- **Fall:** Pumpkin spice, apple cider vibes
- **Winter:** Peppermint, hot cocoa
- **Valentine's:** Love-themed packaging

### 4. Gloss of the Month Club
- Monthly subscription
- New or exclusive flavor each month
- Special members-only packaging

### 5. Custom Labels
- Customer's name on the gloss tube
- "Made for [Name]" - perfect for gifts!

### 6. Best Friend Duo Pack
- Two glosses, two thank you cards
- "One for you, one for your bestie"
- Matching friendship stickers

### 7. Review Rewards
- Leave a review = sticker or small discount
- Feature customer photos on social media

### Platform Options
| Platform | Pros | Cons | Cost |
|----------|------|------|------|
| Etsy | Easy setup, built-in shoppers | Fees add up, lots of competition | ~$0.20/listing + 6.5% fees |
